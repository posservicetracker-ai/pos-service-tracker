## About

**POS Service Tracker** is built for POS agents, shop owners and marketers in Nigeria.

It helps you track daily **Airtime, Data, Transfer, Withdrawal, Deposit, Bills & Electricity** transactions in one place — even offline.

Know your exact profit, charges, VAT and balance every day.

- Works without data. Survives shutdown. When electricity goes off, your data is safe.
- 14 providers + Others: Moniepoint, OPay, PalmPay + 11 more.
- Auto VAT (7.5% only for Transfer), Stamp Duty, Profit, E-float.

## Features

- 📱 **Offline-First** - Works without data. Syncs when online.
- 🔒 **Survives Shutdown** - Phone off, app killed, battery dead — data restored.
- 💰 **Auto VAT & Stamp Duty** - VAT = Fee x 7.5% only for Transfer & Transfer-Agent. No VAT for Airtime, Data, Deposit, Bills, Withdrawal.
- 📊 **Daily Profit Report** - See profit, charges, balance at a glance.
- 🏦 **14 Providers + Others** - Moniepoint, OPay, PalmPay + 11 more.
- 📥 **Install in 10 secs** - PWA, no Play Store needed. Auto-fit desktop / tablet / phone.
